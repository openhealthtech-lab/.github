## About

Open HealthTech Collab Lab builds hands-on projects and teaching material on top of trusted open-source tools in healthcare and biotech. We work with **academia, research centres and hospitals** to turn real problems into student projects, prototypes and shared open resources.

## Focus areas

| Area | What we work on | Built on |
|---|---|---|
| 🩻 **Medical imaging AI** | Segmentation, classification, AI-assisted labelling of scans | [MONAI](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAI) |
| 🧬 **AI for drug discovery & biology** | Protein and molecule models, virtual screening, AI agents for science | [NVIDIA BioNeMo](https://github.com/NVIDIA-BioNeMo) |
| 🎓 **Teaching resources** | Lab sheets, notebooks and project briefs for diploma and final-year students | Public datasets, Google Colab |

## What you'll find here

- **Teaching labs**: step-by-step notebooks that run on free tools such as Google Colab
- **Project briefs**: ideas scoped for Year 1–3 and final-year projects (FYPs)
- **Collab projects**: prototypes built with partner organizations
- **Contributions**: fixes and examples we send back to the projects we build on

## Work with us

We're looking for partners who want to:

- **Co-supervise student projects** on a real problem from your team
- **Pilot a prototype**, for example AI-assisted labelling or image quality checks
- **Share expertise** through guest talks, datasets or problem statements

Get in touch at **contact.devicedr@gmail.com**.

## Data & ethics

- We use **public, de-identified datasets** by default.
- **No patient data** is ever stored in these repositories.
- Any work with partner data runs under a written data-sharing agreement.
- We respect the licenses of every project we build on.
