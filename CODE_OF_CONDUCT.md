# Code of Conduct

Open HealthTech Collab Lab brings together students, educators, researchers and clinicians. We want everyone to feel welcome, whatever their background or level of experience.

## Our standards

We expect everyone taking part to:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners and students
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Credit other people's work, data and ideas
- Focus on what is best for the community and for patients

We do not accept:

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language of any kind
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- **Sharing patient data or any identifiable health information** in issues, pull requests, discussions or files

## Scope

This code applies in all of the organization's repositories, discussions and events, and when someone represents the lab in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **contact.devicedr@gmail.com**. Every report is reviewed and handled in confidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code. They may also temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.
