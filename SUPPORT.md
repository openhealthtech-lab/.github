# Getting Help

## Questions about our material

- **Something is broken or unclear**: open an issue using the *Bug report* template.
- **General questions**: use the repo's Discussions tab, if it is turned on.

## Questions about the tools we build on

- **MONAI**: [documentation](https://monai.readthedocs.io) and [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAI/discussions)
- **NVIDIA BioNeMo**: [BioNeMo on GitHub](https://github.com/NVIDIA-BioNeMo)

## Collaborations

To discuss a partnership, guest talk or co-supervised project, open a *Collaboration proposal* issue or email **contact.devicedr@gmail.com**.

## Not medical advice

Nothing in these repositories is medical advice or a clinical tool.
